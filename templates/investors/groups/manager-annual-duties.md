# What a group's manager must do each year

**Legallas is an AI agent for legal work, not a lawyer or law firm. Using it creates no attorney-client relationship. AI can be wrong. Have your own lawyer review before you sign, file or rely on anything. No lawyer has reviewed this.**

## In one screen

A group whose members invest individually has light yearly duties: keep the entity in good standing, refresh member
statements, and keep the deal process and fee rules current. A group whose manager runs vehicles (one-deal vehicles, a
sidecar or a pooled fund) takes on a small fund's duties for each vehicle: Form D amendments while an offering
continues, an exempt reporting adviser's yearly Form ADV update (relying on the venture capital fund exemption, 203(l),
or the private fund adviser exemption under $150 million, 203(m): [17 CFR 275.204-4(a)][e204-4]), Colorado's exemption renewal, partnership tax returns
and Schedule K-1s, and records showing each vehicle still meets its exemptions. Set reminders for every line below that
applies.

## Drafting notes

### Choice: who keeps the calendar

**Recommendation:** name one person in the charter as responsible for filings, with a second person who checks. Keep the
calendar in the group's own copy of Legallas, with reminders set from the dates in this file.

### Choice: running vehicles at all

**Recommendation:** count the yearly lines above before starting a vehicle program. For each vehicle, the manager takes
on at least a Form D, state notices, a Schedule K-1 run and the Form ADV update. **Where a lawyer should look:** whether
the manager can rely on the venture capital fund exemption for every vehicle, especially any that buys shares from
existing holders.

## What couldn't be verified

- The SEC's Form ADV instructions couldn't be opened from this session (sec.gov refused the fetch on 2026-11-06), as in the
  sibling files. The 60-day first filing is General Instruction 13 as secondary copies give it; 17 CFR 275.204-4 sets no date.
- Rule 51-4.11(IA) was read through a legal research database. It leaves the fee amount to the Securities Commissioner,
  so no figure is given here.
- Delaware's annual tax is $400 for an LLC and $100 for each registered series in the current statute. The 2026 act that raised it took effect
  on 2026-01-01 ([85 Del. Laws c. 273, section 45][hb400]), but doesn't say which June 1 payment first owes the new amount.
