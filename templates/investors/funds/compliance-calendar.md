# The fund's compliance calendar

**Legallas is an AI agent for legal work, not a lawyer or law firm. Using it creates no attorney-client relationship. AI can be wrong. Have your own lawyer review before you sign, file or rely on anything. No lawyer has reviewed this.**

## In one screen

These are the dates a venture fund manager, relying on an exempt reporting adviser route, with a calendar fiscal year,
should have in the calendar. Dates that come from a statute or rule cite it. Dates that come from the partnership
agreement (`{{lpa_reporting}}`) are marked contractual: they bind because the manager promised them.

## Yearly calendar (fiscal year ending December 31)

| When | What | Source |
|---|---|---|
| January to March | Prepare Schedule K-1s; collect portfolio valuations for year-end statements | Practice |
| By March 15 | Fund's partnership return and Schedule K-1s to each partner: Form 1065 is due "by the 15th day of the 3rd month following the date its tax year ended" (IRS, read 2026-11-07). Or file Form 7004 for an automatic six-month extension (K-1s then follow the extended date) | [26 U.S.C. 6072(b)][us6072]; [26 CFR 1.6081-2][e6081]; [1.6031(b)-1T][e6031]; [Form 1065 instructions][i1065] |
| By March 31 (within 90 days after year end) | Adviser's annual updating amendment to Form ADV, as an exempt reporting adviser, with the fee | [17 CFR 275.204-1, 275.204-4][e204-4]; [Form ADV instructions][adv-i] |
| As the partnership agreement says, often 90 to 120 days after year end | Audited financial statements to limited partners | Contractual |
| With the fund's Colorado return | Colorado composite return and tax for nonresident individual partners, unless a partner files the agreement that excludes them. Have your own tax professional review it | [C.R.S. 39-22-601(5.5)][t39] |
| By June 1 | Delaware annual tax for each Delaware fund, general partner and management company entity: "in the amount of $400", due "on June 1 following the close of the calendar year" (an LLC's on "the first day of June"); $100 for each registered series. Paid late, a limited partnership "shall pay the sum of $200", added to the tax | [6 Del. C. 17-1109(a), (b), (d)][d17s11]; [18-1107][d18s11], read 2026-11-07 |
| By the last day of the second month after each anniversary month (`{{anniversary_months}}`) | Colorado periodic report for each Colorado entity, and each Delaware entity registered in Colorado | [C.R.S. 7-90-501][t7] |
| Quarterly, as the partnership agreement says | Quarterly reports and capital account statements | Contractual |
| Yearly | Code of ethics and insider trading acknowledgments from staff; pay-to-play certifications | Policy |
| With each exempt reporting adviser filing | The same report filed with Colorado through the federal system, with the fee the Securities Commissioner sets, to keep Colorado's private fund adviser exemption | [3 CCR 704-1, Rule 51-4.11(IA)][ccr] |

## Event-driven dates

| When | What | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Within 15 days after the first sale | Form D | [17 CFR 230.503][e503] |
| Same deadline, in each state where an investor lives | State notice filings; Colorado through the national system | [3 CCR 704-1, Rule 51-3.7][ccr] |
| On or before each anniversary while the offering continues | Form D amendment, federal and Colorado | [17 CFR 230.503][e503]; [Rule 51-3.7][ccr] |
| Before each closing | Refresh bad actor questionnaires; check the holder count and the qualifying venture capital fund cap, if used | [17 CFR 230.506(d)][e506]; [Investment Company Act exemptions](investment-company-act.md) |
| Before each closing or transfer | Benefit plan investor percentage, if relying on staying under 25% | [29 CFR 2510.3-101(f)][e2510] |
| Within the fund's yearly valuation period (no more than 90 days) | If relying on the venture capital operating company route: at least 50% of assets at cost in venture capital investments, and management rights actually exercised during the year | [29 CFR 2510.3-101(d)][e2510] |
| At each investment | Record that it is a qualifying investment, or count it in the 20% basket; check borrowing | [17 CFR 275.203(l)-1][e203l] |
| Before any political contribution by the adviser or a covered associate, or before soliciting a government investor | Pay-to-play check and log | [17 CFR 275.206(4)-5][e206-5]; proposed for rescission on 2026-09-10, still in force |
| Promptly when certain Form ADV facts change | Other-than-annual amendment | [Form ADV instructions][adv-i] |
| Each capital call and distribution | Notices to limited partners as the partnership agreement says | Contractual |
| From 2028-01-01 | Anti-money laundering program for registered and exempt reporting advisers | [31 CFR 1032.210][fincen] |

## Drafting notes

### Choice: who owns the calendar

**Recommendation:** name a compliance lead (often the chief operating or financial officer in a small manager) and a
second person who checks every filing. Keep evidence of each filing with the date.

### Choice: outside help

**Recommendation:** use a fund administrator for capital accounts and a fund tax preparer for Schedule K-1s from the first
year. The deadlines in this file don't move for a small fund. **Where a lawyer should look:** the first Form ADV, the
first Form D, and any government or benefit plan investor.

## What couldn't be verified

- The Form ADV fee, the exact items an exempt reporting adviser answers, and the amendment triggers come from secondary
  copies of the SEC's instructions; the instructions couldn't be opened from this session.
- Delaware's $400 annual tax is in the current statutes. The 2026 act that raised it from $300 put those sections into effect
  on 2026-01-01 ([85 Del. Laws c. 273, section 45][hb400]), but doesn't say which June 1 payment first owes $400. The
  Division's own instructions, read 2026-11-06, say "$400" and that "the annual taxes for the prior year are due on or before
  June 1st" ([Division's entity tax instructions][detax]); its FAQ still says $300 in one answer.
