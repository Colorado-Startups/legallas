# Limited partnership agreement outline

**Legallas is an AI agent for legal work, not a lawyer or law firm. Using it creates no attorney-client relationship. AI can be wrong. Have your own lawyer review before you sign, file or rely on anything. No lawyer has reviewed this.** The partnership agreement is the fund's most
important document; a fund lawyer drafts it.

## In one screen

The limited partnership agreement sets the fund's economics (fees, carried interest, distributions), governance (who
decides, what limited partners can block, how the general partner is removed) and the terms that keep the fund inside
its legal exemptions. This outline lists each section, what it decides, the usual options, a recommendation for a first
venture fund, and where a lawyer should look. It is not a draft agreement. The public models (the Institutional Limited Partners Association's, written for buyout
funds, and VC Lab's Cornerstone set) are useful reading but may not be copied ([ILPA][ilpa-lpa]; [VC Lab][vclab]).

| 14 | Removal of the general partner | For cause, or without cause by a supermajority | | For cause with a stated vote; consider no-fault removal at a high threshold |

## Drafting notes

### Choice: how far to limit the general partner's duties

| Fund state | What the law allows |
|---|---|
| Delaware | The agreement may expand, restrict or eliminate duties, except it may not eliminate the implied covenant of good faith and fair dealing ([6 Del. C. 17-1101][d17s11]) |
| Colorado | A general partner has a general partner's duties under Colorado's partnership law: article 60 unless the partnership elects article 64 ([C.R.S. 7-62-403, 7-62-1104][t7]). Under article 64, the agreement can't eliminate the loyalty duties, but can name categories of activity that don't breach them if not manifestly unreasonable, or let partners approve a transaction after full disclosure; it can't unreasonably reduce the duty of care or eliminate good faith and fair dealing ([7-64-103][t7]) |

**Recommendation:** limit duties to what investors will accept and the state allows: keep liability for fraud, bad
faith, wilful misconduct and gross negligence, and route conflicts through the advisory committee. Under any state's law,
an adviser's anti-fraud duties to the fund and its investors remain ([17 CFR 275.206(4)-8][e206-8]).

**Where a lawyer should look:** the duty language, exculpation and indemnification; for a Colorado fund, which
partnership article governs.

### Choice: whole-fund or deal-by-deal carry

**Recommendation:** whole-fund. It is what most limited partners expect and avoids paying carry on early winners that
later losses wipe out. **Where a lawyer should look:** the clawback, its tax-netting, and who guarantees it. The carried
interest holding period rule applies either way ([26 U.S.C. 1061][us1061]).

### Choice: the venture capital fund representation

**Recommendation:** state the strategy in the agreement and the offering materials, and track the 20% basket. Losing
venture fund status forces the manager onto the $150 million private fund route or registration. **Where a lawyer should
look:** secondary purchases, fund-of-funds investments and bridge loans, which can fall in the basket.
