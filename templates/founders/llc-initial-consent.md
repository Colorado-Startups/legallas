# Initial Consent of the Members (Colorado or Delaware LLC)

> Legallas is an AI agent for legal work, not a lawyer or law firm. Using it creates no attorney-client relationship. AI can be wrong. Have your own lawyer review before you sign, file or rely on anything.
>
> No lawyer has reviewed this template.

## Before you use this

Use this right after the filing, to record the first decisions in the members' own names. A corporation splits these among the
incorporator, the board and the stockholders. An LLC needs one page signed by its members.

**The state-law rules:**

| | Colorado | Delaware |
|---|---|---|
| The filing | Articles of organization, which must say whether members or managers manage ([7-80-204(1)(e)][t7]) | Certificate of formation, signed by an authorized person ([18-201(a)][d6s2]) |
| Who must sign | **Every member.** The Act's section on member action without a meeting ([7-80-711][t7]) is repealed. Amending the operating agreement and acting outside the ordinary course take every member ([7-80-401(2)][t7]), and so does admitting a member after the filing ([7-80-701(1)][t7]) | Members with the votes a meeting of all members would need, unless the agreement says otherwise ([18-302(d)][d6s3]). Admitting a member after formation takes every member unless the agreement says otherwise ([18-301(b)(1)][d6s3]) |
| The operating agreement | May be signed before, at or after the filing, effective at formation ([7-80-108(1)(c)][t7]) | The same ([18-201(d)][d6s2]). Delaware calls it the limited liability company agreement |
| Managers | Designated by a majority of members ([7-80-402][t7]) | Only if the agreement provides for them. Otherwise members manage by share of profits ([18-402][d6s4]) |
| Yearly state tax | Not covered here | $400 ([18-1107(b)][d6s11]), due June 1 ([18-1107(c)][d6s11]) |

**Have every member sign,** whichever state. Colorado needs it, and it's the cleanest record in Delaware. If managers manage, each
manager signs too.

**Sign the operating agreement the same day:** [Colorado, one member](colorado-llc-single-member-operating-agreement.md),
[Colorado, two or more members](colorado-llc-operating-agreement.md), [Delaware, one member](delaware-llc-agreement.md), or
[Delaware, two or more members](delaware-llc-operating-agreement.md). Before
issuing Units that can be repurchased, read Section 6.9 of the two-member agreement on the tax election.

## Signing checklist

- Every member, and every manager if managers manage, signs and dates it. The Company records the date it received each one.
- The members sign the operating agreement the same day.
- Each member and officer who works for the Company signs the [invention assignment](invention-assignment.md).
- In Delaware under the second signing option, each member who didn't sign gets a copy.

## Drafting notes

| Choice | Options | Recommendation and why | Where a lawyer should look |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who signs | Every member; the votes needed | **Every member.** Colorado needs it: the section on action without a meeting ([7-80-711][t7]) is repealed, and admitting members and amending the agreement take every member ([7-80-401(2)][t7], [7-80-701(1)][t7]). Delaware allows the votes needed unless the agreement says otherwise ([18-302(d)][d6s3]), but one page signed by everyone is the cleanest record | Whether the operating agreement sets its own consent procedure |
| Ratifying the filing (1) | Include; leave out | **Include.** It records that the members accept the filing and the steps taken to make it | None |
| Adopting the agreement (2) | Here too; only in the agreement | **Here too.** Both states let the agreement take effect as of formation ([7-80-108(1)(c)][t7], [18-201(d)][d6s2]), and the consent records the date | None |
| Initial members (3) | List them here; rely on the agreement | **List them here.** In Delaware an initial member is admitted at the later of formation and the time the agreement or the records set ([18-301(a)][d6s3]), and this list is a record | None |
| Securities record (3) | Include; leave out | **Include.** A Unit can be a security: federal and Colorado law both list "investment contract" ([15 U.S.C. 77b(a)(1)][us77b]; [C.R.S. 11-51-201(17)][t11]). If it is one, the exemptions are the same as for founder stock: "transactions by an issuer not involving any public offering" ([77d(a)(2)][us77d]) and, in Colorado, "Any transaction not involving any public offering" ([11-51-308(1)(i)][t11]). The [initial board consent](initial-board-consent.md) records founder stock the same way. Recording the facts costs nothing, whichever way the question comes out | Whether the Units are securities, which turns on the facts; any member outside Colorado |
| Management (4) | Members; managers | **Match the filing and the agreement.** Colorado articles must say which ([7-80-204(1)(e)][t7]), and a majority of members designates managers ([7-80-402][t7]). In Delaware, members manage by share of profits unless the agreement provides for managers ([18-402][d6s4]). Who is an agent follows the choice in Colorado ([7-80-405][t7]) | Officer authority letters for banks |
| Tax classification (7) | Default; corporation; S corporation | **Default.** One member is disregarded and two or more are a partnership ([26 CFR 301.7701-3(b)(1)][e7701]). An election uses Form 8832 ([301.7701-3(c)(1)(i)][e7701]), and a timely S election counts as one ([301.7701-3(c)(1)(v)(C)][e7701]) | Have your own tax professional review any election before it's filed |
| Acts before formation (10) | Adopt a list; adopt everything | **A list.** Adopting only what's listed keeps unknown obligations out of the Company | Whether a person who signed before formation stays personally bound, which wasn't researched |

## What couldn't be checked

- **Colorado's yearly report.** C.R.S. title 7, article 90, wasn't read for this template, so the Colorado due date isn't stated.
- **Contracts signed before formation.** Whether the signer stays personally liable after the Company adopts one wasn't researched in either state.
- **Whether Units are securities** turns on the facts and case law, which weren't read. Neither were the Colorado Securities Commissioner's rules or another state's law. The two-member Colorado agreement adds investment confirmations in its Section 2.5.
