# Section 83(b) Election, with company copy and filing record

## Before you use this

A founder whose stock or LLC capital units can be forfeited until they vest files this to be taxed at the transfer, not as they vest.
Without it, tax is generally worked out when the property vests, on its value then ([26 U.S.C. 83(a)][us83]).

| Rule | The law's or the IRS's words, read 2026-11-13 | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Deadline | "shall be made not later than 30 days after the date of such transfer" | [26 U.S.C. 83(b)(2)][us83] |
| No undo | "Such election may not be revoked except with the consent of the Secretary" | [26 U.S.C. 83(b)(2)][us83] |
| Weekend or holiday | "the election will be considered timely filed if it is postmarked by the next succeeding day which is not a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday" | [Form 15620 instructions][f15620]; [26 U.S.C. 7503][us7503] |
| Where | "filing one copy of a written statement with the internal revenue office with which the person who performed the services files his return" | [26 CFR 1.83-2(c)][e83-2] |
| Tax return | No copy of the election goes with the tax return. The rule asks only for "one copy of a written statement" filed "with the internal revenue office with which the person who performed the services files his return" | [26 CFR 1.83-2(c)][e83-2], as eCFR reads today |
| Company copy | "shall also submit a copy of the statement referred to in paragraph (c) of this section to the person for whom the services are performed" | [26 CFR 1.83-2(d)][e83-2] |
| Postmark | "the date of the United States postmark stamped on the cover in which such return, claim, statement, or other document, or payment, is mailed shall be deemed to be the date of delivery". It counts only if the statement was "deposited in the mail in the United States in an envelope or other appropriate wrapper, postage prepaid, properly addressed to the agency, officer, or office with which the return, claim, statement, or other document is required to be filed" | [26 U.S.C. 7502(a)(1), (a)(2)(B)][us7502] |
| Registered mail | "such registration shall be prima facie evidence that the return, claim, statement, or other document was delivered" | [26 U.S.C. 7502(c)(1)(A)][us7502] |
| Later forfeiture | "if such property is subsequently forfeited, no deduction shall be allowed in respect of such forfeiture". The regulation still allows a loss for what was paid: the forfeiture "shall be treated as a sale or exchange upon which there is realized a loss equal to the excess (if any) of" "(1) The amount paid (if any) for such property, over, (2) The amount realized (if any) upon such forfeiture." "If such property is a capital asset in the hands of the taxpayer, such loss shall be a capital loss." | [26 U.S.C. 83(b)(1)][us83]; [26 CFR 1.83-2(a)][e83-2] |
| Profits interests | "Taxpayers to which this revenue procedure applies need not file an election under section 83(b) of the Code." | [Rev. Proc. 2001-43][rp2001], section 3 |

| Day | Step |
|---|---|
| 0 | The transfer: the Purchase Date in the [founder stock purchase agreement](founder-stock-purchase.md), or the date units issue under the operating agreement |
| As soon as possible | Sign Part 1. Give Part 2 and a copy of Part 1 to the company, then mail Part 1 to the IRS |
| By day 30 | The postmark. A last day on a weekend or legal holiday moves to the next business day |
| After | Fill in Part 3 and keep it, the receipt and the copies in the room. Nothing goes with the tax return |

Count day 30 from the transfer date. The instructions to [Form 15620][f15620] say to mail the form; this statement goes the same way.

**Example, invented.** Doe buys 6,000,000 shares of Harbor Labs, Inc. common stock at $0.0001 a share, $600.00 in all, on Friday 2027-01-08. The shares vest
over four years, and the company can buy back unvested shares if Doe leaves. Value equals price, so item 7 is $0.00. Day 30 is Sunday 2027-02-07, so a postmark
on Monday 2027-02-08 is still on time. Doe mails it on 2027-01-12 by certified mail and files the receipt in the room as DOE-0042.

## Drafting notes

| Choice | Options | Recommendation and why | Where a lawyer or tax professional should look |
|---|---|---|---|
| Form | This statement; IRS Form 15620 | **Either.** The form's instructions say an election "may be made by filing a written statement that satisfies the requirements of Treas. Reg. § 1.83-2" ([Form 15620][f15620]). Part 1 holds each item 1.83-2(e) lists | Whether an online route suits (not checked) |
| Mailing | Registered or certified mail; ordinary mail | **Registered or certified, with a receipt.** Registration is "prima facie evidence" of delivery ([7502(c)(1)][us7502]) | The certified mail and private carrier rules in the regulations, not read here |
| When | Within days; on day 30 | **Within days of the transfer.** The deadline has no extension, and a late election can't be fixed | |
| Company copy first | Before mailing; after | **Before.** Item 8 says copies "have been furnished", the words of 1.83-2(e)(7) | |
| Price | Equal to value; below value | **Equal to value at formation,** so item 7 is zero. A price below value is income now | How value was set |
| Profits interest units | Protective filing; no filing | **Ask the tax professional.** Rev. Proc. 2001-43 says such holders "need not file" when its conditions hold | Its conditions, and Rev. Proc. 93-27 |
| Taxpayer identification number | Written by hand; filled in | **By hand.** The AI never needs the number | |

## What couldn't be checked

- **An online route.** Form 15620 (Rev. 4-2025) says to mail it. The IRS page listing an online route did not load its list.
- **Which IRS address.** The Form 1040 "Where to file" table, which sets the "Mail to" address, wasn't read.
- **26 CFR 301.7502-1** (certified mail, private carriers) and **Rev. Proc. 93-27** weren't read.
- **State income tax** treatment wasn't read.
