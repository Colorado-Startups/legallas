# Action of the Incorporator

> Legallas is an AI agent for legal work, not a lawyer or law firm. Using it creates no attorney-client relationship. AI can be wrong. Have your own lawyer review before you sign, file or rely on anything.
>
> No lawyer has reviewed this template.

## Before you use this

Use this right after the articles or certificate are filed, if they don't name the first directors. The incorporator adopts bylaws and elects the board, then steps away.

- **Colorado:** the incorporators may adopt initial bylaws and elect a board ([7-102-105(1)(a), -106(1)][t7]), and may act without a meeting in the
  way directors do ([7-102-105(2), 7-108-202][t7]).
- **Delaware:** the incorporator manages the company until directors are elected, and may adopt bylaws and elect directors ([107, 108, 109(a)][d8s1]).
- **An LLC doesn't use this.** Instead, its members sign the [initial LLC member consent](llc-initial-consent.md) and the operating agreement: [one member](colorado-llc-single-member-operating-agreement.md) or [two or more](colorado-llc-operating-agreement.md) in Colorado, or [one member](delaware-llc-agreement.md) in Delaware.
- **Next:** the [initial board consent](initial-board-consent.md).

## The document

<!-- TEMPLATE START -->

**ACTION OF THE INCORPORATOR OF {{COMPANY_NAME}}**

The undersigned, the sole incorporator of {{COMPANY_NAME}}, a {{STATE_OF_INCORPORATION}} corporation (the "Company"), takes these actions without
a meeting. The Company's charter document was filed on {{FILING_DATE}}.

**1. Bylaws.** The bylaws attached as Exhibit A are adopted as the Company's initial bylaws.

**2. Board size.** The bylaws set the number of directors, and this action elects that number.

**3. Directors.** These individuals are elected as directors, to serve until the first annual meeting of shareholders or stockholders and until their
successors are elected and qualify, or until their earlier death, resignation or removal: {{DIRECTOR_NAMES}}.

**4. Incorporator's role ends.** The incorporator has taken no other action for the Company. The incorporator's powers end on this election, and
the incorporator resigns.

Signed on {{SIGNING_DATE}}.

______________________________
{{INCORPORATOR_NAME}}, Incorporator

Exhibit A: Bylaws

## Drafting notes

| Choice | Options | Recommendation and why | Where a lawyer should look |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who is the incorporator | A founder; a lawyer or filing service | **A founder is fine.** If a service acts, get this signed the same day: until directors are elected, the incorporator has the power to elect them | A service's engagement terms |
| Board size | One; two; three | **An odd number if you can.** With two founders, a two-seat board deadlocks on any disagreement; the [founders' agreement](founders-agreement.md) must then supply the way out. A three-seat board with an agreed independent third director avoids it. One founder: one seat | Who holds a third seat, and who can remove them |
| Who sits on the board | All founders; only some | **Every founder holding a meaningful stake,** with the seat protected by a [voting agreement](founders-agreement.md). A board seat is where a founder sees what's coming | Investor seats later |
