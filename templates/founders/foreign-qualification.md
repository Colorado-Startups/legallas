## Before you use this

A company formed outside Colorado, such as a Delaware corporation, files this statement before doing business in Colorado. The filing is the Secretary of State's online form; this worksheet gathers its answers first.

| Rule | The law's or the Secretary of State's words, read 2026-11-13 | Source |
|---|---|---|
| When | "A foreign entity shall not transact business or conduct activities in this state except in compliance with this part 8 and not until its statement of foreign entity authority is filed" | [C.R.S. 7-90-801(1)][crs7] |
| Not doing business, examples | "(c) Maintaining bank accounts"; "(e) Selling through independent contractors"; "(k) Conducting an isolated transaction that is completed within thirty days and that is not one in the course of repeated transactions of a like nature"; "(l) Transacting business or conducting activities in interstate commerce" | [7-90-801(2)][crs7] |
| The list | "The list of activities in subsection (2) of this section is not exhaustive." | [7-90-801(3)][crs7] |
| Without it: courts | Not "permitted to maintain a proceeding in any court in this state for the collection of its debts" until the statement is filed | [7-90-802(1)(a)][crs7] |
| Without it: fees | "not to exceed one hundred dollars for each calendar year or part of a calendar year" | [7-90-802(2)][crs7] |
| Without it: penalty | "a civil penalty, payable to this state, not to exceed five thousand dollars" | [7-90-802(3)][crs7] |
| Its acts still stand | "does not impair the validity of the acts of the foreign entity or prevent it from defending any proceeding in this state" | [7-90-802(5)][crs7] |
| Fee | "Foreign Entity Authority Statement": online $100.00; paper n/a | [Fee schedule][sosfees], "Revised July 1, 2024" |
| How | "A foreign entity can obtain authority to transact business in Colorado by filing a Statement of Foreign Entity Authority with the Colorado Secretary of State." | [Foreign entities FAQ][sosfor], A2 |
| Name taken | "the foreign entity will be required to choose an assumed entity name for use in Colorado" | [Foreign entities FAQ][sosfor], A5 |
| Agent | "An entity appointed to serve as a registered agent must have a usual place of business in Colorado" | [Foreign entities FAQ][sosfor], A4 |

After it files, the company is a reporting entity and files a [Colorado periodic report](colorado-periodic-report.md) every year.

## Drafting notes

| Choice | Options | Recommendation and why | Where a lawyer should look |
|---|---|---|---|
| When to file | Before the first Colorado hire or lease; after | **Before.** Without it, the company can't sue in Colorado to collect its debts, and fees and penalties can build up | Whether a remote employee alone is enough |
| Line (g) date | Expected start; actual start | **The real date.** The statute asks for the date it "commenced or expects to commence" | |
| Registered agent | A commercial agent; a person in Colorado | **A commercial agent,** for a steady address | |
| Other states | File each where the company does business | **Same test in each state.** Delaware: an LLC registers "Before doing business in the State of Delaware" ([6 Del. C. 18-902][del6sc9]); a corporation follows [8 Del. C. 371(b)][del8sc16] | Each state's own test |
