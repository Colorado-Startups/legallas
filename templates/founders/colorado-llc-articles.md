## Before you use this

| | |
|---|---|
| **It fits** | A Colorado LLC. Read the [comparison](entity-comparison.md) first. A Delaware LLC files a [certificate of formation](delaware-llc-certificate.md) instead |
| **Filing** | Online with the Colorado Secretary of State. Articles of Organization for a limited liability company: "$50.00" online, no paper option ([fee schedule][sosfees], "Revised July 1, 2024", read 2026-11-13). The live form wasn't opened, because the site's terms bar robots |
| **A person files it** | The Secretary of State's terms prohibit robots, so the product prepares this and you press submit |
| **No signature** | The person who causes delivery affirms, under penalty of perjury, that it's the company's act, the facts are believed true and it complies with the law ([7-90-301.5][t7]). Their true name and mailing address go on it ([7-90-301(8)][t7]) |
| **Who forms it** | One or more persons, each individual 18 or older. They "need not be members" after formation ([7-80-203(1)][t7]) |
| **When it exists** | When the articles become effective ([7-80-207][t7]) |
| **Must match** | Article 6 and Section 3.1 of the [operating agreement](colorado-llc-operating-agreement.md) say who manages. Pick the same option in both |
| **Then** | The operating agreement ([two or more members](colorado-llc-operating-agreement.md) or [one member](colorado-llc-single-member-operating-agreement.md)), then the [initial consent](llc-initial-consent.md). Each year, the [periodic report](colorado-periodic-report.md) |

## Drafting notes

| Choice | Options | Recommendation and why | Where a lawyer should look |
|---|---|---|---|
| Management (6) | A, members; B, managers | **Match Section 3.1 of the operating agreement.** The articles must say which ([7-80-204(1)(e)][t7]). Changing later takes articles of amendment, approved by all members unless the operating agreement says otherwise ([7-80-209(1.5), (5)][t7]). Amending is "$25.00" online ([fee schedule][sosfees]) | What a member of a manager-managed company may still sign |
| Other matters | Leave out; add | **Leave out.** The articles may add "Any other matters" the persons forming the company choose ([7-80-204(1)(h)][t7]). Anything added is public and takes a filing to change. Votes, Units and duties go in the operating agreement | None |
| Persons forming (4) | A founder; a lawyer or filing service | **A founder,** unless someone else files. That person needn't stay a member ([7-80-203(1)][t7]) | None |

**Name.** It must contain "limited liability company", "ltd. liability company", "limited liability co.", "ltd. liability co.",
"limited", "l.l.c.", "llc", or "ltd." ([7-90-601(3)(c)][t7]). It must be distinguishable on the Secretary of State's records
([7-90-601(2)][t7]). A name that clears the Secretary of State can still infringe a trademark.

**Registered agent.** The company must keep one in Colorado for as long as its articles are on file ([7-90-701(1)][t7]). It can be an
individual 18 or older whose primary residence or usual place of business is in Colorado. That person holds a Colorado driver's license or ID
card, or confirms residency with the Secretary of State under its policies. It can also be an entity in good standing with a usual place of business here, including the company
itself ([7-90-701(1), (2)][t7]). The filing must say the agent consented ([7-90-701(3)][t7]). The agent's address becomes public. Changing
the agent takes a statement of change, "$10.00" online ([fee schedule][sosfees]).
