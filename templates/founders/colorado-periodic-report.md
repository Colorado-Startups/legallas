## Before you use this

Every Colorado entity, and every out-of-state company with Colorado authority, files a short report with the Secretary of State each year. This record sets the month, checks the answers and keeps the proof.

| Rule | The law's or the Secretary of State's words, read 2026-11-13 | Source |
|---|---|---|
| What it states | The entity name, the jurisdiction where formed, "(c) The registered agent name and registered agent address" and "(d) The principal office address" | [C.R.S. 7-90-501(1)][crs7] |
| From July 2027 | "(f) Beginning July 1, 2027, if the reporting entity is an employer, EEO-1 data." | [7-90-501(1)(f)][crs7] |
| Employer | A private entity in Colorado "that employs one hundred or more workers and that, as of March 1, 2026, is required to submit EEO-1 data" | [7-90-501(9)(b)(I)][crs7] |
| First report | Due "no later than the last day of the second calendar month following the first anniversary of the calendar month" the formation document or foreign authority took effect | [7-90-501(4)(c)(I)][crs7] |
| Current facts | "Information in the periodic report shall be current as of the date the periodic report is delivered" | [7-90-501(4)(d)][crs7] |
| How | "The Periodic Report must be filed electronically through our website." | [Periodic report FAQ][sosrep], A2 |
| Window | "The Periodic Report can be filed two months prior to the Periodic Report month or two months after without any penalty." | [Periodic report FAQ][sosrep], A4 |
| Reminder | "an email notice that a Periodic Report is due will be sent the month prior to the Periodic Report month", for those signed up | [Periodic report FAQ][sosrep], A5 |
| Late, then delinquent | "If the Periodic Report Month is January, the report due date will be March 31st. If the Periodic Report is not filed on or before March 31st, the entity's status will become Noncompliant, and a late report will be due by May 31st. If the Periodic Report is not filed on or before May 31st, the entity's status will be changed to Delinquent." | [Delinquency FAQ][sosdel], A1 |
| The name | "The delinquent entity name is saved for the entity for 400 days from the date of delinquency." | [Delinquency FAQ][sosdel], A2 |
| Fees | "Periodic Report" $25.00; "Periodic Report Late Filing Penalty" $50.00; "Statement Curing Delinquency" $100.00; all online | [Fee schedule][sosfees], "Revised July 1, 2024 - Subject to change without notice." |

**Example, invented.** Harbor Labs, Inc. gains Colorado authority effective 2027-02-15, so its report month is February. The first report may go in from December 2027 and is due by 2028-04-30. If missed, the status turns Noncompliant and a late report is due by 2028-06-30. After that it turns Delinquent. Doe files on 2028-02-03 for $25.00, and the receipt is filed as DOE-0051.

## Drafting notes

| Choice | Options | Recommendation and why | Where a lawyer should look |
|---|---|---|---|
| When to file | Early in the window; near the end | **The first week of the report month.** It is free and the facts are fresh | |
| Annual or biennial | Annual; the biennial election in 7-90-501(4)(c)(III) | **Annual,** unless the Secretary of State's conditions for biennial are read and suit | The biennial election's conditions |
| Changes mid-year | Wait for the report; file a statement of change | **File the statement of change then.** Each is $10.00 online on the fee schedule | |
| EEO-1 line | Skip; answer each year | **Answer each year from July 2027.** The test is 100 or more workers and a federal EEO-1 duty | Whether the company is an employer under (9)(b) |
| Delinquent | Cure fast; let it lapse | **Cure within 400 days,** or the name changes and another company may take it | C.R.S. 7-90-901 to 7-90-904 |
