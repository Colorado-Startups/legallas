## Before you use this

The answers to gather before filing in the USPTO's own online system. Legallas fills this worksheet; a person files. Run the
[clearance search](trademark-clearance.md) first.

**The company is the applicant.** "An application filed in the name of an entity that did not own the mark as of the filing date of
the application is void." And "the application cannot be amended to set forth a different entity as the applicant" ([37 CFR 2.71(d)][e271]).
A founder who files in their own name for a brand the company owned on the filing date gets a void application; the company files again.

## Drafting notes

| Choice | Options | Recommendation and why | Where a lawyer should look |
|---|---|---|---|
| Applicant | Company; a founder | **Company.** A filing by a non-owner is void and can't be moved to another entity ([37 CFR 2.71(d)][e271]) | Who owned the mark on the filing date, if a founder used it first |
| Basis | A, use; B, intent to use | **A once selling across state lines; B before launch.** Use in commerce means "selling or transporting your goods out of state or in providing services to customers who live outside your state" ([Filing basis][basis]) | The dates of first use |
| Goods description | ID Manual entry; free-form text | **ID Manual.** Free text adds $200.00 per class ([fee schedule][fees]) | A description too narrow for the business |
| Classes | Fewest that cover current goods; many | **Fewest.** Each class adds $350.00 ([37 CFR 2.6(a)(1)(iii)][e26]) | Planned products |
