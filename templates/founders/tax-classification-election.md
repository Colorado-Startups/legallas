## Before you use this

A record of how the company chose its federal tax treatment, and proof the form went in on time. The form itself is the IRS's: [Form 8832][f8832] or [Form 2553][f2553].

| Rule | The law's or the IRS's words, read 2026-11-13 | Source |
|---|---|---|
| LLC default | "(i) A partnership if it has two or more members; or (ii) Disregarded as an entity separate from its owner if it has a single owner." | [26 CFR 301.7701-3(b)(1)][e7701-3] |
| Form 8832 timing | "can not be more than 75 days prior to the date on which the election is filed and can not be more than 12 months after the date on which the election is filed" | [301.7701-3(c)(1)(iii)][e7701-3] |
| 60 months | "cannot change its classification by election again during the sixty months succeeding the effective date of the election". But "An election by a newly formed eligible entity that is effective on the date of formation is not considered a change" | [301.7701-3(c)(1)(iv)][e7701-3] |
| Who signs 8832 | "(A) Each member of the electing entity who is an owner at the time the election is filed; or (B) Any officer, manager, or member of the electing entity who is authorized" | [301.7701-3(c)(2)(i)][e7701-3] |
| Form 2553 timing | "at any time during the preceding taxable year, or" "at any time during the taxable year and on or before the 15th day of the 3d month of the taxable year" | [26 U.S.C. 1362(b)(1)][us1362] |
| Counting it | "No more than 2 months and 15 days after the beginning of the tax year the election is to take effect". The IRS's example: a first tax year starting January 7 runs to March 21 | [Form 2553 instructions][i2553] |
| Who consents to S | "valid only if all persons who are shareholders in such corporation on the day on which such election is made consent to such election" | [1362(a)(2)][us1362] |
| S limits | A small business corporation must not "(A) have more than 100 shareholders, (B) have as a shareholder a person (other than an estate, a trust described in subsection (c)(2), or an organization described in subsection (c)(6)) who is not an individual, (C) have a nonresident alien as a shareholder, and (D) have more than 1 class of stock." | [26 U.S.C. 1361(b)(1)][us1361] |
| LLC choosing S | An eligible entity filing Form 2553 "doesn't need to file Form 8832" | [Form 2553 instructions][i2553] |
| Ending S | Revoked "only if shareholders holding more than one-half of the shares" consent. A new election waits until the "5th taxable year" unless the IRS consents | [1362(d)(1)(B), (g)][us1362] |
| Where, principal business in Colorado | Form 8832: "Ogden, UT 84201". Form 2553: the Ogden, UT 84201 service center, or "Fax: 855-214-7520". The IRS adds "The filing information shown above is subject to change." | [Form 8832][f8832] mailing update; [Form 2553 instructions][i2553] |

**Example, invented.** Harbor Labs LLC, a Colorado LLC, admits Doe and a cofounder on 2027-01-08. Its default is a partnership. The members plan a fund investment within a year, so they keep the default under Option A. Had they chosen Option C for a tax year starting 2027-01-08, the 2 months would end 2027-03-07 and the last day would be 2027-03-22. The record is filed as DOE-0047.

## Drafting notes

| Choice | Options | Recommendation and why | Where a lawyer or tax professional should look |
|---|---|---|---|
| Which option | A, B or C | **A, unless the accountant shows a saving.** B can't change again for 60 months. Ending C needs more than half the shares, then a 5-year wait to elect again | Self-employment tax, Colorado tax, a planned conversion |
| S status and funds | Choose S now; wait | **Wait, if a fund investment is planned.** A fund that is a partnership is a shareholder that "is not an individual" ([1361(b)(1)(B)][us1361]) | Whether any planned investor fits the list |
| LLC choosing S | Form 2553 alone; Form 8832 then 2553 | **Form 2553 alone.** The instructions say no Form 8832 is needed | |
| Effective date (B) | Formation date; later | **Formation date** for a new LLC, so the 60-month limit doesn't start | |
| How to send | Certified mail; fax (Form 2553) | **Either, with proof.** Keep the receipt or fax confirmation, and the original | |
| Missed deadline | Late relief; wait a year | **Ask the tax professional.** Late relief rules exist (Rev. Proc. 2013-30, 2009-41) | Those revenue procedures |
