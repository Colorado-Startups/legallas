# Rule 701 Grant Record, 12-month totals and disclosure

> Legallas is an AI agent for legal work, not a lawyer or law firm. Using it creates no attorney-client relationship. AI can be wrong. Have your own lawyer review before you sign, file or rely on anything.
>
> No lawyer has reviewed this template.

## Before you use this

A running record of equity granted to employees, directors, officers and consultants under SEC Rule 701, updated with every grant. It grants nothing: the written plan or contract does that, and a lawyer reviews those.

| Rule | The rule's words, read 2026-11-13 | 17 CFR 230.701 |
|---|---|---|
| Who may use it | "any issuer that is not subject to the reporting requirements of section 13 or 15(d) of the Securities Exchange Act" | [(b)(1)][e701] |
| What it covers | Grants "under a written compensatory benefit plan (or written compensation contract)" for "employees, directors, general partners, trustees (where the issuer is a business trust), officers, or consultants and advisors" | [(c)][e701] |
| Consultants | "(i) They are natural persons"; "(ii) They provide bona fide services"; "(iii) The services are not in connection with the offer or sale of securities in a capital-raising transaction" | [(c)(1)][e701] |
| Options | "sales of securities underlying options must be counted as sales on the date of the option grant" | [(d)(1)][e701] |
| The limit | In "any consecutive 12-month period" sales "must not exceed the greatest of" "(i) $1,000,000"; "(ii) 15% of the total assets of the issuer"; "(iii) 15% of the outstanding amount of the class", each at "the issuer's most recent balance sheet date (if no older than its last fiscal year end)" | [(d)(2)][e701] |
| Pricing | "Options must be valued based on the exercise price of the option." "The value of services exchanged for securities issued must be measured by reference to the value of the securities issued." | [(d)(3)(i)][e701] |
| Outstanding | "treat the securities underlying all currently exercisable or convertible options, warrants, rights or other securities, other than those issued under this exemption, as outstanding" | [(d)(3)(iii)][e701] |
| Always delivered | "a copy of the compensatory benefit plan or the contract, as applicable" | [(e)][e701] |
| Over $10 million | If the 12-month total "exceeds $10 million", disclosure goes out "a reasonable period of time before the date of sale": plan summary, risks and financial statements "no more than 180 days before the sale" | [(e)(1) to (4)][e701] |
| Resale | "deemed to be “restricted securities”" | [(g)(1)][e701] |
| State law | "the issuer also must comply with any applicable state law relating to the offer and sale of securities" | [Preliminary Note 2][e701] |
| A miss | "may claim any other exemption that is available" | [Preliminary Note 3][e701] |

**Example, invented.** Harbor Labs, Inc.'s balance sheet of 2027-12-31 shows total assets of $250,000 and 10,000,000 common shares outstanding. Test (ii) is $37,500 and test (iii) is 1,500,000 shares. In 2028 it grants options on 400,000 shares at $0.05, an aggregate sales price of $20,000. The 12-month total stays under $1,000,000, so test (i) holds. The record is filed as DOE-0050.

## Drafting notes

| Choice | Options | Recommendation and why | Where a lawyer should look |
|---|---|---|---|
| Comparing the tests | Price against (i) and (ii), number against (iii); one unit for all | **Record both price and number,** and show each test | How the three tests compare when units differ |
| When to update | Every grant; each quarter | **Every grant.** Options count on the grant date, and the 12 months roll | |
| LLC units and profits interests | Value at grant; zero | **Ask the lawyer.** Services are valued "by reference to the value of the securities issued" | Valuing a profits interest under (d)(3)(i) |
| Nearing $10 million | Plan disclosure early; react | **Plan early.** The financial statements must be no more than 180 days old at each sale | Rule 701(e)(4) and Form 1-A Part F/S |
| State law | Check each grant; once | **Each grant, by state of the holder.** The rule leaves state law in place | Each holder's state exemption |
