# Trademark Clearance Search Record

> Legallas is an AI agent for legal work, not a lawyer or law firm. Using it creates no attorney-client relationship. AI can be wrong. Have your own lawyer review before you sign, file or rely on anything.
>
> No lawyer has reviewed this template.

## Before you use this

A record of the search a company runs before it adopts or files a name, logo or slogan. File the result with the
[trademark filing worksheet](trademark-filing.md).

| Step | The USPTO's words, read 2026-11-09 | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Why | "One of the most common reasons applications are rejected is because the applied-for trademark is too similar to an already registered one and there's a likelihood of confusion" | [Why search][why] |
| When | "Before you apply to register a trademark, conduct a clearance search" | [Why search][why] |
| Where | "our trademark database of federally registered and applied-for trademarks, state trademark databases, and the internet" | [Why search][why] |
| Who else looks | The examining attorney searches only "our federal database". "It's your responsibility to search state trademark databases and the internet" | [Why search][why] |

- **The legal test.** A mark is refused if it so resembles a registered mark, or one used earlier and not abandoned, "as to be likely, when used on or in connection with the goods of the applicant, to cause confusion" ([15 U.S.C. 1052(d)][us1052]).
- **Search for the company, in the company's name.** A filing in the wrong owner's name is void ([37 CFR 2.71(d)][e271]).

## Drafting notes

| Choice | Options | Recommendation and why | Where a lawyer should look |
|---|---|---|---|
| How wide to search | USPTO only; USPTO, states and web | **All three.** The USPTO names all three, and its examiner checks only the federal database ([Why search][why]) | Unregistered users with earlier rights |
| Paid full search | Own search; a search firm's report | **Own search first.** A paid report adds common-law sources. Get one when the mark is core to the brand | Whether the budget justifies one |
| Decision | A; B; C | **A only when Section 2 has nothing close.** Likelihood of confusion turns on both the marks and the goods ([15 U.S.C. 1052(d)][us1052]) | Any row marked close |

## What couldn't be checked

- **The USPTO search system's own help pages** were not read. The search page needs a browser with JavaScript.
- **State trademark registries,** Colorado's included, were not opened.
- **Common-law rights** (use without registration) can't be found by any one database.
