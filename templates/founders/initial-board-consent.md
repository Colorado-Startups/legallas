# Initial Consent of the Board of Directors

> Legallas is an AI agent for legal work, not a lawyer or law firm. Using it creates no attorney-client relationship. AI can be wrong. Have your own lawyer review before you sign, file or rely on anything.
>
> No lawyer has reviewed this template.

## Before you use this

This is the first thing the board does, by written consent instead of a meeting. It adopts the bylaws if the incorporator didn't, picks
officers, issues founder stock and sets up the company's basic records.

**The state-law rules:**

| | Colorado | Delaware |
|---|---|---|
| Board consent | Every director must consent in writing, unless the bylaws require a meeting ([7-108-202(1)][t7]). It takes effect when the last director signs, unless the directors set another date ([7-108-202(2), (3)][t7]) | Every director must consent, in writing or by electronic transmission ([141(f)][d8s4]) |
| Stock price | The board must determine that the consideration is adequate, before issuing ([7-106-202(3)][t7]) | Consideration is set by board resolution, at least par ([152, 153][d8s5]) |
| A note for shares | Doesn't count unless it's negotiable and secured by collateral **other than the shares** ([7-106-202(5)][t7]) | The board decides the form of consideration ([152(a)][d8s5]). A lawyer should check any note |
| Conflicts | Each founder is on both sides. The sale stands if approved after disclosure by disinterested directors or shareholders, or if it's fair ([7-108-501(2)][t7]) | Section 144's safe harbours, rewritten in 2025 ([144][d8s4]) |

**The conflict.** Every director here is usually also buying stock, so no disinterested director is left to approve the sale. Three things help:
every founder pays the same price per share, all facts are stated in the consent, and the [stockholder consent](initial-stockholder-consent.md)
ratifies it. A lawyer should confirm that this is enough for your facts, especially if founders get different numbers of shares or if one
contributes more than the others.

**What must happen next:**

- **The 83(b) election: each founder has 30 days from the stock transfer.** It's an election to be taxed now on stock that vests later, so growth in value isn't taxed as it vests. Calendar it the day shares are issued. It can't be fixed later ([26 U.S.C. 83(b)][us83]).
- Sign the [founder stock agreements](founder-stock-purchase.md) and [invention assignments](invention-assignment.md).
- Get the [stockholder consent](initial-stockholder-consent.md) signed once shares are issued.
- Apply for an EIN, then open the bank account.
- Delaware company: file Colorado foreign authority before transacting business here ([7-90-801][t7]).

An LLC doesn't use this. Instead, its members sign the [initial LLC member consent](llc-initial-consent.md) and the operating agreement: [one member](colorado-llc-single-member-operating-agreement.md) or [two or more](colorado-llc-operating-agreement.md) in Colorado, or [one member](delaware-llc-agreement.md) in Delaware.

Resolved: The Company shall sell and issue to the following people the shares of common stock shown, at ${{PRICE_PER_SHARE}} per share, on the terms of a

Resolved: The board has determined that the consideration for these shares is adequate, and that the fair value of the common stock today is

Resolved: The board has considered the terms above and determined that these sales are fair as to the Company and its stockholders.

Resolved: Shares of the Company's stock are uncertificated. The Secretary shall give each holder the notice or information statement the law requires,

**4. Record of the securities exemption**

Resolved: These shares are offered and sold without registration, relying on section 4(a)(2) of the Securities Act of 1933, 15 U.S.C. 77d(a)(2), as a

Company engaged in no general solicitation and paid no commission; and each buyer is taking the shares for investment and accepts a restrictive legend or
notice. An officer shall make any state filing that applies to a buyer outside Colorado.

Resolved: The Invention Assignment in the form attached as Exhibit B is approved. Each founder, and each employee and contractor who may create anything for

## Signing checklist

- Every director signs. Colorado and Delaware both require all of them for a board consent.
- The founder stock table shows each buyer's name, share count, total price and vesting.
- All exhibits are attached and match the versions being signed.
- Any director's interest beyond the stock sale is disclosed in section 3.
- The incorporator's bylaws are the ones in the minute book.

## Drafting notes

| Choice | Options | Recommendation and why | Where a lawyer should look |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price per share | Par or a nominal price | **The same nominal price for every founder, set at formation before anything of value is in the company.** A low price paid early is what keeps the 83(b) tax small. For Delaware, it can't be below par ([153][d8s5]). Stock bought later, or after a term sheet, may be worth more, and the difference is taxable | Whether anything of value is already in the company; whether contributed technology changes the price |
| What founders pay with | Cash; technology; a note | **Cash for the price, plus the [invention assignment](invention-assignment.md).** Avoid a note in Colorado: it must be negotiable and secured by something other than the shares ([7-106-202(5)][t7]). Stock issued for services isn't treated as issued for property under [26 U.S.C. 351](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t26/s351/), the rule that lets owners move property into a corporation they control without tax, and that can change the tax on formation | How the technology is valued; whether section 351 applies |
| Uncertificated shares | Uncertificated; certificates | **Uncertificated.** Fewer lost papers. Colorado: [7-106-207][t7]; Delaware: [158][d8s5]. Either way, a transfer restriction binds a person without knowledge only if it's noted on the certificate or the required information statement ([7-106-208(2)][t7]; [202(a)][d8s6]) | The information statement or notice |
| Who is CEO | Name one; co-CEOs | **Name one** and record the deal in the [founders' agreement](founders-agreement.md). In Colorado, the board can remove an officer unless the bylaws differ ([7-108-303(4)][t7]). In Delaware, the bylaws or the board decide ([142(b)][d8s4]), and the template bylaws state it. In Colorado, the title gives no contract right ([7-108-304][t7]) | Any employment agreement |
| Equity plan now or later | A, now; B, later | **Now if you'll hire within a year.** It's cheap to adopt, but no option can be priced until you've met the deferred pay tax rule, section 409A, which in practice means setting a supportable fair market value ([1.409A-1(b)(5)(iv)][e409a]). Stockholders must approve within 12 months for incentive stock options ([422(b)(1)][us422]) | Rule 701 limits ([17 CFR 230.701][e701]) and the Colorado plan exemption ([11-51-307(1)(i)][t11]) |
| Securities record | Keep it; skip | **Keep it.** There's no filing for a founder sale under section 4(a)(2), so this record is your evidence for the exemption ([15 U.S.C. 77d(a)(2)][us77d]; [11-51-308(1)(i)][t11]). What counts as no public offering is judge-made. Colorado also has two separate exemptions with set numbers: offers to at most 20 people and sales to at most 10 buyers in Colorado in any 12 consecutive months, where the seller reasonably believes the buyers are buying for investment and no commission or other pay goes to anyone for soliciting a buyer except a licensed broker-dealer or sales representative, not counting institutional investors ([11-51-308(1)(j)][t11]); and, before the company exists, subscriptions from at most 25 people, with no payment and no commission or other pay for soliciting ([11-51-308(1)(k)][t11]) | A founder or buyer outside Colorado needs that state's rule |

## What couldn't be checked

- **Whether the conflict approach is enough** for your facts. The fairness route exists in Colorado ([7-108-501(2)(c)][t7]); Delaware's section 144 was rewritten in 2025, and how it applies at formation wasn't researched.
- **Securities exemption facts** are yours to confirm. This records them; it doesn't make them true.
