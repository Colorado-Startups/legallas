# LLC Member Ledger

> No lawyer has reviewed this template.

## Before you use this

The LLC's record of its members, managers, contributions and every change to them. It holds what a member may ask to see, in one place.
Schedule A of the [Colorado operating agreement](colorado-llc-operating-agreement.md) or the [Delaware LLC agreement](delaware-llc-agreement.md) shows today's holdings; this ledger adds the history.

| Item | Colorado: a member may inspect, read 2026-11-13 | Delaware: a member may obtain, read 2026-11-13 |
|---|---|---|
| Who | "A current list of the name and last-known business, residence, or mailing address of each member and manager" ([C.R.S. 7-80-408(1)(c)][crs7]) | "A current list of the name and last known business, residence or mailing address of each member and manager" ([18-305(a)(3)][del6sc3]) |
| Contributions | "True and full information regarding the amount of cash and a description and statement of the agreed value of any other property or services contributed by each member and that each member has agreed to contribute in the future, and the date on which each became a member" ([7-80-408(1)(e)][crs7]) | The same, in nearly the same words ([18-305(a)(5)][del6sc3]) |

| Rule | Colorado | Delaware |
|---|---|---|
| Duty to keep the list | 7-80-408 lists the records. Later sections call them "records required to be kept by section 7-80-408" ([7-80-502(1)][crs7]) and "records required to be kept pursuant to section 7-80-408" ([7-80-503, 7-80-504][crs7]). The contribution values "as stated in" those records set the cash a member may owe for an unmade contribution, and each member's share of profits, losses and distributions unless the operating agreement says otherwise ([7-80-108(1)(a)][crs7]) | "A limited liability company shall maintain a current record that identifies the name and last known business, residence or mailing address of each member and manager." ([18-305(h)][del6sc3]) |
| Electronic | Allowed "if such form is capable of conversion into written form within a reasonable time" ([7-80-408(4)][crs7]) | Allowed "if such form is capable of conversion into paper form within a reasonable time" ([18-305(d)][del6sc3]) |
| A member's demand | "shall be in writing and shall state the purpose of the demand" ([7-80-408(5)][crs7]) | "shall be in writing and shall state the purpose of such demand" ([18-305(e)][del6sc3]) |
| Reply time | Not set in 7-80-408 | "within 5 business days (or such shorter or longer period of time as is provided for in a limited liability company agreement but not longer than 30 business days)" ([18-305(f)][del6sc3]) |
| Keeping secrets back | Members or managers may withhold trade secrets "for such period of time as the members or managers deem reasonable" ([7-80-408(3)][crs7]) | The manager may, under [18-305(c)][del6sc3] |

## Drafting notes

| Choice | Options | Recommendation and why | Where a lawyer should look |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ledger or Schedule A alone | Both; Schedule A alone | **Both.** Schedule A shows today; the ledger shows each change and its approval | Whether the agreement names one as controlling |
| Property and services | Agreed value written down; left blank | **Written down, with who agreed.** Both statutes speak of the "agreed value" | Tax treatment of a contribution of services |
| Promised contributions | In a signed writing; informal | **Signed writing.** In Colorado, "No promise by a member to contribute to the limited liability company is enforceable unless set out in a writing signed by the member" ([7-80-502(3)][crs7]). The Colorado operating agreement's section 2.2 says the same | |
| Reply window | Statute's default; set in the agreement | **Delaware: the 5 business day default.** The agreement may set another period up to 30 | |

## What couldn't be checked

- **Colorado reply time** for a member's demand: none found in 7-80-408. The operating agreement may set one.
- **18-305(g)**, which limits what a member may obtain to what is "necessary and essential" to the purpose, was read only in part.
