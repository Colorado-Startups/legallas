## Before you use this

The agreement is Common Paper's [Mutual NDA 1.0](https://commonpaper.com/standards/mutual-nda/1.0), a cover page plus standard terms, free to use and change under CC BY 4.0. Legallas ships it [unmodified and pinned by SHA-256](../bundled-forms.md#pinned-files). This record is what the company decides and keeps: which blanks it filled, what it changed, and whether the signed copy is on file. Keep Common Paper's attribution line on the agreement.

| Cover page blank | What to decide |
|---|---|
| Purpose | How the other side may use what it learns. Narrow beats broad |
| Effective Date | The day both sign, or an earlier day both agree |
| MNDA Term | Expires after a number of years, or continues until ended |
| Term of Confidentiality | A number of years from the Effective Date, or in perpetuity. The cover page's first choice extends protection for a trade secret until it stops being one |
| Governing law and jurisdiction | A state, and the courts of a city or county |
| Modifications | Anything changed. List each, so the copy says it differs from the standard terms |

| Point | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Trade secret law | A trade secret needs reasonable efforts to keep it secret. An NDA is one such effort. Colorado's act is at C.R.S. 7-74-101 to 7-74-110 and Delaware's at 6 Del. C. 2001 to 2009. Sections named here, not quoted |
| Individuals | A contract with an employee, contractor or consultant that governs trade secrets or confidential information should carry the notice of immunity for confidential disclosure to the government or in a sealed court filing, 18 U.S.C. 1833(b). A company-to-company NDA doesn't need it. Check the section's current text |
| Employees | Don't use this for a hire. A hire signs the company's own confidentiality and [invention assignment](invention-assignment.md) terms |
| Investors | Many investors refuse to sign an NDA before a first meeting. A company that insists can lose the meeting |

**Example, invented.** Harbor Labs, Inc. meets a possible reseller, North Pier Supply LLC, to talk about a pilot. Purpose: evaluate a reseller arrangement for the Harbor platform. Effective 2027-02-01, expires after 1 year, confidentiality 2 years, with trade secrets protected until they stop being secret. Governing law Delaware; courts located in New Castle County. Modifications: none. The signed copy is filed as DOE-0112.

## What couldn't be checked
