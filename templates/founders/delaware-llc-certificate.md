## Before you use this

| | |
|---|---|
| **It fits** | A Delaware LLC. Read the [Delaware LLC comparison](delaware-llc-comparison.md) first. A Colorado LLC files [articles of organization](colorado-llc-articles.md) instead |
| **Registered agent** | Required, with a registered office in Delaware ([18-104(a)][d6s1]). You don't need to be in Delaware. An agent must be generally open or present there to accept service of process, and forward it ([18-104(e)(1)][d6s1]) |
| **Agent contact** | The company gives its agent "the name, business address and business telephone number of a natural person" authorized to receive the agent's communications, and keeps it current ([18-104(g)][d6s1]) |
| **Filing** | Upload a PDF to the Division of Corporations or mail it. The fee is "$110.00" ([Division fee schedule][defee], "Revised August 1, 2026"). Of that, "$70" is the statute's filing fee ([18-1105(a)(3)][d6s11]). The schedule says the fee "equals filing fee plus municipality fee". The portal has a human check (a CAPTCHA), so a person files it, or the registered agent does |
| **Signing** | One or more authorized persons sign ([18-201(a)][d6s2], [18-204(a)][d6s2]). Signing is "an oath or affirmation, under the penalties of perjury in the third degree" that the facts are true ([18-204(d)][d6s2]). An electronic signature works ([18-206(a)][d6s2]) |
| **When it exists** | On filing, or at a later date or time the certificate names ([18-201(b)][d6s2]), up to the 180th day after filing ([18-206(b)][d6s2]) |
| **Then, in Colorado** | Before transacting business here, a [statement of foreign entity authority](foreign-qualification.md) ([7-90-801(1)][t7]) |
| **Every June 1** | An annual tax "in the amount of $400" ([18-1107(b)][d6s11]), due "on the first day of June following the close of the calendar year" ([18-1107(c)][d6s11]). See the [annual tax record](delaware-llc-annual-tax.md) |
| **Then** | The agreement ([two or more members](delaware-llc-operating-agreement.md) or [one member](delaware-llc-agreement.md)), then the [initial consent](llc-initial-consent.md) |

## Drafting notes

| Choice | Options | Recommendation and why | Where a lawyer should look |
|---|---|---|---|
| What it says | The two required items; add management, purpose or other terms | **Only the two.** It must state the name and the registered office and agent, and may add "Any other matters the members determine to include therein" ([18-201(a)][d6s2]). Unlike Colorado's articles, it needn't say who manages, so the [agreement](delaware-llc-operating-agreement.md) decides. Anything added is public, and a certificate of amendment costs "$220.00" ([Division fee schedule][defee], "Revised August 1, 2026"). Of that, "$180" is the statute's filing fee ([18-1105(a)(3)][d6s11]) | Any term an investor wants on the public record |
| When it takes effect | On filing; a later date | **On filing,** unless a contract or tax year calls for a later start. A later date can be at most 180 days after filing ([18-206(b)][d6s2]) | None |
| Registered agent | A service; a Delaware resident; the company itself | **A service** for a company run from Colorado. The company can serve itself only if it keeps an office generally open in Delaware ([18-104(a)(2), (e)(1)][d6s1]) | None |
| Address form | Street, number, city and postal code | **All four.** A registered office or agent address "shall include the street, number, city and postal code" ([18-104(k)][d6s1]) | None |

**Name.** It must contain "Limited Liability Company", "L.L.C." or "LLC" ([18-102(1)][d6s1]). It must be distinguishable on the Secretary
of State's records ([18-102(3)][d6s1]). A name that clears Delaware can still infringe a trademark.
