## Before you use this

A Delaware LLC pays a flat tax by June 1 each year and files no annual report. The risk is that nobody owns the task. This record names who pays, who backs them up, and where the proof goes.

| Rule | The law's or the Division's words, read 2026-11-13 | Source |
|---|---|---|
| The tax | "shall pay an annual tax, for the use of the State of Delaware, in the amount of $400" | [6 Del. C. 18-1107(b)][del6sc11] |
| When | "due and payable on the first day of June following the close of the calendar year or upon the cancellation of a certificate of formation" | [18-1107(c)][del6sc11] |
| Interest | "the tax shall bear interest at the rate of 1½% for each month or portion thereof until fully paid" | [18-1107(c)][del6sc11] |
| The statement | Mailed "at least 60 days prior to June 1" "in care of its registered agent in the State of Delaware" | [18-1107(d)][del6sc11] |
| Late | Unpaid on or before June 1: "shall pay the sum of $200" | [18-1107(e)][del6sc11] |
| Standing | Unpaid when due: "shall cease to be in good standing" | [18-1107(h)][del6sc11] |
| Restored | On "payment of the annual tax and all penalties and interest thereon for each year" | [18-1107(i)][del6sc11] |
| Courts | Out of good standing: "may not maintain any action, suit or proceeding in any court of the State of Delaware" until restored | [18-1107(l)][del6sc11] |
| Contracts | Non-payment "shall not impair the validity of any contract, deed, mortgage, security interest, lien or act" | [18-1107(m)][del6sc11] |
| Members | A member or manager "is not liable" for the LLC's debts "solely by reason of" non-payment | [18-1107(n)][del6sc11] |
| Three years | The certificate of formation "shall be canceled if the annual tax" "is not paid for a period of 3 years from the date it is due" | [18-1108(a)][del6sc11] |
| No report | LLCs "do not file an Annual Report". "Penalty for non-payment or late payment is $200.00. Interest accrues on the tax and penalty at the rate of 1.5% per month." | [Franchise tax page][corpfr] |
| Scam notices | "The Secretary of State’s Office works closely with law enforcement to investigate deceptive solicitations." | [Pay taxes page][corppay] |

**Example, invented.** Had Harbor Labs LLC been a Delaware LLC formed on 2027-01-08, its first $400 would be due 2028-06-01. Doe pays and a cofounder is the backup. The registered agent forwards the statement in March 2028, and Doe pays online on 2028-05-04. The receipt is filed as DOE-0053.

## Drafting notes

| Choice | Options | Recommendation and why | Where a lawyer should look |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who pays | A member; the registered agent; an accountant | **One named person and one backup.** No report reminds anyone, and the statement goes to the agent | |
| Reminder date | June 1; a month earlier | **May 1.** Late adds $200 and interest at once | |
| Standing lapses | Pay at once; wait | **Pay at once.** Until restored, the LLC can't sue in a Delaware court | Whether a pending suit is affected |
| Series LLC | Track the LLC only; each series too | **Each registered series too.** 18-1107(b) adds $100 per registered series | Protected and registered series |
