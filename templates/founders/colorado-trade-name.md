## Before you use this

A company that does business in Colorado under any name but its exact legal name files a statement of trade name. The filing is the Secretary of State's online form; this worksheet gathers its answers first.

| Rule | The law's or the Secretary of State's words, read 2026-11-13 | Source |
|---|---|---|
| When | "a person shall not transact business in this state under a name other than the true name of the person" "unless an effective statement of trade name is on file in the records of the secretary of state" | [C.R.S. 7-71-101][crs7] |
| Without it: courts | No proceeding "for the collection of a debt from another with whom or with which the person transacted business" under that name, until the statement is filed | [7-71-102(1)][crs7] |
| Without it: penalty | "a civil penalty not to exceed five hundred dollars" | [7-71-102(2)][crs7] |
| Its acts still stand | "does not impair the validity of the acts of the person at any time taken" | [7-71-102(3)][crs7] |
| How long | For reporting entities, it "shall remain effective in perpetuity", subject to delinquency and dissolution. For others, through "the last day of the twelfth calendar month" unless renewed | [7-71-104(1)][crs7] |
| Reporting entity | A Colorado entity on file, and "any foreign entity authorized to transact business or conduct activities in this state" | [7-90-102(58)][crs7] |
| Renewal | During "the last three calendar months the statement of trade name is effective", for a person other than a reporting entity | [7-71-105(1)][crs7] |
| Delinquent | "the trade name remains effective for one year from the date of Delinquency or Dissolution" | [Trade names FAQ][sostn], A9 |
| Liability | Liable for business under the trade name "to the same extent and in the same manner as if the business were transacted under its true name" | [7-71-104(2)][crs7] |
| How | "A Statement of Trade Name must be filed and paid for online." | [Trade names FAQ][sostn], A7 |
| Fees | "Trade Name Statement" $20.00; "Trade Name Renewal" $5.00; "Change of Trade Name Information" $10.00; "Trade Name Withdrawal" $10.00; all online | [Fee schedule][sosfees], "Revised July 1, 2024" |

A Delaware company files its [statement of foreign entity authority](foreign-qualification.md) first. It then counts as a reporting entity. The filing is no trademark search or registration: the [trademark clearance record](trademark-clearance.md) covers that.

## Drafting notes

| Choice | Options | Recommendation and why | Where a lawyer should look |
|---|---|---|---|
| When to file | Before first use; after | **Before.** Until it is on file, the company can't sue in Colorado to collect debts from those it dealt with under the name | |
| Product names | File each; file none | **File each name the company sells or invoices under.** Whether a product name alone needs one wasn't checked | Which names count |
| Delaware company | Authority first; trade name alone | **Authority first.** It then counts as a reporting entity, and the trade name lasts without yearly renewal | |
| The description | Broad; narrow | **Plain and short,** as the statute asks for "a brief description" | |
