# Management Rights Letter

> Legallas is an AI agent for legal work, not a lawyer or law firm. Using it creates no attorney-client relationship. AI can be wrong. Have your own lawyer review before you sign, file or rely on anything.
>
> No lawyer has reviewed this template.

## Before you use this

Only if a fund asks, or a term sheet promises one: Series Seed's term sheet promises one to each Major Purchaser. A fund that takes money from pension plans may need to be a "venture capital operating company" so its assets aren't treated as plan assets. To be one, it needs "contractual rights directly between the investor and an operating company to substantially participate in, or substantially influence the conduct of, the management of the operating company" ([29 CFR 2510.3-101(d)(3)(ii)][e2510]). This letter gives that right and nothing more.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Who signs** | The company, to one fund. The fund countersigns |
| **When** | At the closing of the fund's investment |
| **Gives** | Consultation, inspection and information. No vote, no veto and no board seat |

## The document

<!-- TEMPLATE START -->

**MANAGEMENT RIGHTS LETTER**

{{SIGNING_DATE}}

To {{INVESTOR_NAME}} (the "Investor"):

This letter confirms that, in addition to any other rights it has as a holder of shares of {{COMPANY_NAME}} (the "Company"), the Investor has these rights while it holds any shares of the Company:

1. **Consultation.** The Investor may consult with and advise the Company's management on significant business matters, at reasonable times and on reasonable notice. Management shall make itself available for this at least once each calendar quarter.

2. **Inspection and information.** The Investor may inspect the Company's books and records and its facilities at reasonable times and on reasonable notice, and shall receive the financial statements and other information the Company gives its board, other than what this letter allows the Company to withhold.

[OPTION A: No board attendance. Recommended unless the Investor has no seat and insists.]
3. **No board rights.** This letter gives no right to attend board meetings.
[END OPTION A]

[OPTION B: The Investor's representative may attend board meetings when the Investor has no director.]
3. **Board meetings.** If no director represents the Investor, the board shall consider in good faith the Investor's advice on significant matters, and one representative of the Investor may attend board meetings as a non-voting observer, other than any part the board closes to protect privilege, confidential information of others, or the Company's position on a matter in which the Investor has a conflict of interest.
[END OPTION B]

4. **Limits.** The Company may withhold any information or access that would risk losing legal privilege, breach a duty to someone else, or, in the board's reasonable judgment, harm the Company if the Investor or its affiliates compete with it. The Investor shall keep everything it receives under this letter confidential, and use it only to monitor its investment. Nothing in this letter limits the Investor's legally protected right to report to a government agency.

5. **No control.** These rights don't let the Investor direct the Company, approve or block any action, or bind it.

6. **Ending.** These rights end when the Investor no longer holds shares of the Company, when the Company's securities are first sold in a registered public offering, or when the Company sells all or substantially all its assets or merges, or its stock is sold, so that the people who held its voting stock just before hold less than a majority of the voting power of the Company or its successor.

7. **General.** This letter is governed by the laws of the Company's state of incorporation. Only a writing signed by both can change it.

## Drafting notes

| Choice | Options | Recommendation and why | Where a lawyer should look |
|---|---|---|---|
| Give one at all | Only if a fund asks; always | **Only if a fund asks, or the term sheet promised it.** It exists for the fund's own plan asset question ([2510.3-101(d)][e2510]), not the company's. If using Series Seed's term sheet, strike the promise for Major Purchasers that don't need one | Whether the fund's counsel needs specific words |
| Board attendance (3) | A, none; B, observer when no director | **A.** An observer sees everything the board sees. If the fund insists, B keeps the board's right to close a session | Observers and privilege |
| Withholding (4) | As drafted; none | **As drafted.** A fund may hold stakes in competitors, and privilege can be lost by sharing | None |

**Fights.** The letter gives no vote or veto, so in a fight between founders and investors it adds only information and a meeting a quarter. Section 4 lets the company withhold what would cost it privilege.

## What couldn't be checked

- **What a fund's own counsel treats as enough** to "substantially participate in, or substantially influence" management. The regulation gives no list; this letter gives consultation, inspection and information, which is a judgment, not a safe harbor.
