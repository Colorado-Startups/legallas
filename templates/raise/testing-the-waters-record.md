## Before you use this

Testing the waters means asking the public whether they'd invest before an offering starts. Three SEC rules allow it. Each needs set statements in every communication, and each one is still an offer under the antifraud rules.

| | Rule 241 | Regulation Crowdfunding, Rule 206 | Regulation A, Rule 255 |
|---|---|---|---|
| When | "At any time before making a determination as to the exemption" ([230.241(a)][e241]) | "At any time before the filing of an offering statement" ([227.206(a)][e206]) | "At any time before the qualification of an offering statement" ([230.255(a)][e255]) |
| Antifraud | "deemed to be an offer of a security for sale for purposes of the antifraud provisions" ([241(a)][e241]) | Same words ([206(a)][e206]) | Same words ([255(a)][e255]) |
| No money until | "the issuer makes a determination as to the exemption to be relied on and the offering ... is commenced" ([241(a)][e241]) | "the offering statement is filed" ([206(a)][e206]) | "the offering statement is qualified" ([255(b)(2)][e255]) |
| Response form | May ask "name, address, telephone number, and/or email address" ([241(c)][e241]) | Same ([206(c)][e206]) | Same ([255(c)][e255]) |
| Offering starts | "the date the issuer first made a generic offer soliciting interest" ([230.152(c)(1)][e152]) | The first 206 solicitation or the public Form C, whichever is earlier ([152(c)(4)][e152]) | The first 255 solicitation or the public Form 1-A, whichever is earlier ([152(c)(3)][e152]) |
| Afterwards | A Rule 506(b) buyer who isn't accredited gets "any written communication or broadcast script used under the authorization of § 230.241 within 30 days prior to such sale" ([230.502(b)(2)(viii)][e502]) | Form C includes each 206 communication, and each 241 communication "within 30 days of the initial filing" ([227.201(z)][e201]) | After public filing, materially wrong materials are redistributed, revised ([255(d)][e255]) |

**Colorado.** The 2026 C.R.S. title 11 has no testing-the-waters notice: a search of the title for solicitations of interest found none. Once a federal offering statement is filed but not effective, an offer, "but not a sale", is exempt ([11-51-308(1)(m)][t11]). Colorado's Rule 506 exemption covers offers and sales under section 4(a)(2), and Rule 241 isn't one of them ([308(1)(p)][t11]).

**A later Rule 506(b) round.** For each buyer, the company needs a reasonable belief that it "Did not solicit such purchaser through the use of general solicitation" or "Established a substantive relationship with such purchaser prior to the commencement" of that round ([230.152(a)(1)][e152]).

## Drafting notes

| Choice | Options | Recommendation and why | Where a lawyer should look |
|---|---|---|---|
| Which rule | 241 before choosing; 206 or 255 once the route is known | **The route's own rule once it is known.** Rule 241 material follows the company into a 506(b) round and a Form C | Whether a 506(b) round can still follow |
| Legend wording | The rule's words, as drafted; a paraphrase | **The rule's words.** Each rule says what the communication "must state" | None |
| Keeping copies | Every post, slide and script; a summary | **Every copy.** Rules 201(z) and 502(b)(2)(viii) hand the written ones to buyers | Retention period |
