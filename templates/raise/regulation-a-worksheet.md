## Before you use this

Regulation A is a small public offering: the SEC reviews and qualifies an offering statement on Form 1-A before any sale. A lawyer and an auditor prepare and file it. This worksheet gathers the company's answers and tracks the dates.

| Rule | The law's words, read 2026-11-13 | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Offering plus sales in the prior 12 months "does not exceed $20,000,000" | [230.251(a)(1)][e251] |
| Tier 2 | "does not exceed $75,000,000, including not more than $22,500,000 offered by all selling securityholders that are affiliates" | [230.251(a)(2)][e251] |
| Who can | "organized under the laws of the United States or Canada ... with its principal place of business in the United States or Canada"; not a reporting-history failure; not a bad actor | [230.251(b)][e251]; [230.262][e262] |
| Offers | "Except as allowed by Rule 255 (§ 230.255), no offer of securities may be made unless an offering statement has been filed" | [230.251(d)(1)(i)][e251] |
| Sales | None "Until the offering statement has been qualified" | [230.251(d)(2)(i)(A)][e251] |
| Tier 2 buyers | A "qualified purchaser" for Section 18(b)(3) is "any person to whom securities are offered or sold pursuant to a Tier 2 offering" | [230.256][e256] |
| Tier 2 buyer limit | Unless listed or accredited, a cap of ten percent of income or net worth; the company may rely on the buyer's representation | [230.251(d)(2)(i)(C), (D)][e251] |
| SEC fee | "No fee is payable to the Commission upon either the submission or filing of an offering statement on Form 1-A" | [230.252(b)][e252] |
| Signers | "The issuer, its principal executive officer, principal financial officer, principal accounting officer, and a majority of the members of its board of directors or other governing body" | [230.252(c)][e252] |
| Draft review | A first-time issuer may submit a draft privately; qualification no sooner than "21 calendar days after the public filing" | [230.252(d)][e252] |
| Reports, Tier 1 | Form 1-Z exit report "not later than 30 calendar days after the termination or completion of the offering" | [230.257(a)][e257] |
| Reports, Tier 2 | Form 1-K annual, Form 1-SA semiannual, Form 1-U current, each "within the period specified in" its form | [230.257(b)][e257] |
| Colorado, Tier 1 | The Rule 506 exemption excludes "regulation A" ([C.R.S. 11-51-308(1)(p)][t11]). Regulation A securities "may be registered by coordination", effective after "at least twenty days" on file | [11-51-303(1), (3)(b)][t11] |
| Colorado, Tier 2 | The exclusion "shall only apply to Tier 1 offerings". Notice "at least 21 calendar days prior to the initial sale in this state", with Form U-2 and "The filing fee prescribed by the Securities Commissioner"; effective "for twelve months" | [3 CCR 704-1, Rule 51-3.13(A)][ccr] |
| Colorado fees | Set by the Division ([11-51-707(3)(a)][t11]). Amounts: **Unverified**; the Division's fee table refused automated reads on 2026-11-13 | [Division fee table][fees] |

## Drafting notes

| Choice | Options | Recommendation and why | Where a lawyer should look |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier | Tier 1; Tier 2 | **Tier 2 for buyers in many states.** Colorado registers a Tier 1 offering but takes only a notice for Tier 2. Tier 2 adds ongoing reports ([257(b)][e257]) | Each state's notice rules |
| Regulation A or crowdfunding | Regulation A; [Regulation Crowdfunding](regulation-cf-worksheet.md) | **Crowdfunding up to its $5,000,000 cap.** Regulation A costs a lawyer's and an auditor's time before SEC review | Total cost against the raise |
| Private draft | Submit first; file publicly at once | **Submit first** for a first offering. The staff reviews it before anything is public ([252(d)][e252]) | |
