# Closing Checklist for a Priced Round or SAFE

> Legallas is an AI agent for legal work, not a lawyer or law firm. Using it creates no attorney-client relationship. AI can be wrong. Have your own lawyer review before you sign, file or rely on anything.
>
> No lawyer has reviewed this checklist.

## Before you use this

A closing swaps signed papers for money on one day. This list is what a priced round or a SAFE close hands over, and who makes each item.

| Rule or fee | The law's or the agency's words, read 2026-11-13 | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Delaware good standing | "Good Standing Short form: All Entities Domestic and Foreign $50.00 (per certificate)". The statute's share is the whole fee: "a fee of $50 shall be paid for each certificate" | [Division fee schedule][defee], "Revised August 1, 2026"; [8 Del. C. 391(a)(11)][d8sc18] |
| Colorado good standing | "Certificate of good standing, certificates of fact, certified copies": online "Free" | [Fee schedule][sosfees], "Revised July 1, 2024" |
| Delaware restated certificate | "Restated Certificate of Incorporation - amends": "$214.00", marked "VARIES BASED ON STOCK". Adding authorized shares raises it under [391(a)(2)][d8sc18] | [Division fee schedule][defee], "Revised August 1, 2026" |
| Colorado restated articles | "Amend and Restate Articles of Incorporation": online "$25.00" | [Fee schedule][sosfees], "Revised July 1, 2024" |
| Share notice, Delaware | "Within a reasonable time after the issuance or transfer of uncertificated stock, the registered owner thereof shall be given a notice" | [8 Del. C. 151(f)][d8sc5] |
| Share notice, Colorado | "Within a reasonable time after the issuance or transfer of shares without certificates, the corporation shall send to the shareholder a written statement" | [C.R.S. 7-106-207(2)][crs7] |
| Form D | "no later than 15 calendar days after the first sale of securities in the offering" | [17 CFR 230.503(a)(1)][e503] |

**Example, invented.** Harbor Labs, Inc. closes its Series Seed on 2027-06-01. Doe orders a Delaware short-form good standing for $50.00 the week before, and a free Colorado one for its foreign registration. Items 1 to 18 are ticked by 2027-06-10, and the signed checklist is filed as DOE-0072.

## Drafting notes

| Choice | Options | Recommendation and why | Where a lawyer should look |
|---|---|---|---|
| Legal opinion (12) | None; the company lawyer's opinion | **None unless the investment agreement requires one.** The company pays its own lawyer to give it | The lead investor's term sheet |
| Good standing age (4) | Same week; same month | **Same week.** The certificate speaks only for its date | What the investment agreement says |
| Certificated shares (15) | Book entry with notice; paper certificates | **Book entry with the notice the statute requires.** Paper certificates get lost | The bylaws |

## What couldn't be checked

- **Same-day and 24-hour fees.** The Delaware schedule lists them; the table above gives only the standard fee.
- **Delaware's added fee for new authorized shares** under 391(a)(1) and (2) wasn't computed.
