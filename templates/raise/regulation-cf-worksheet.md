## Before you use this

Regulation Crowdfunding lets a company sell to the public online through one registered portal. The portal files Form C on EDGAR with the company. This worksheet gathers the answers first.

| Rule | The rule's words, read 2026-11-13 | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Cap | Sales in the 12 months before "shall not exceed $5,000,000" | [227.100(a)(1)][e100] |
| Each buyer not accredited | "The greater of $2,500, or 5 percent of the greater of the investor's annual income or net worth, if either the investor's annual income or net worth is less than $124,000"; otherwise "Ten percent ... not to exceed an amount sold of $124,000" | [227.100(a)(2)][e100] |
| One portal | "conducted exclusively through the intermediary's platform" | [227.100(a)(3)][e100] |
| Who can't | A company "not organized under, and subject to, the laws of a State or territory of the United States"; one that "Has no specific business plan"; bad actors under 227.503; one behind on its annual reports | [227.100(b)][e100] |
| File | Form C "prior to the commencement of the offering of securities" | [227.203(a)(1)][e203] |
| Advertising | Only a notice that "directs investors to the intermediary's platform", or a [Rule 206 communication](testing-the-waters-record.md) | [227.204(a), (b)][e204] |
| Changes | Form C/A for "any material changes, additions or updates"; an investor who doesn't reconfirm is cancelled | [227.203(a)(2)][e203]; [227.201(k)][e201] |
| Progress | Form C-U "no later than five business days after each of the dates when the issuer reaches 50 percent and 100 percent of the target offering amount" | [227.203(a)(3)(i)][e203] |
| Each year | Form C-AR "no later than 120 days after the end" of the fiscal year | [227.203(b)(1)][e203] |
| Stopping reports | One of five events, such as "fewer than 300 holders of record" after one annual report; then Form C-TR "within five business days" | [227.202(b)][e202]; [227.203(b)(3)][e203] |
| SEC fee | None found. A text search of Part 227 found no filing fee; the SEC's own fee pages weren't read | [Part 227][e227] |
| Colorado notice | Required if the company "has its principal place of business in this state", filed "when the issuer makes its initial Form C filing"; or if Colorado buyers reach 50%, "in no event later than thirty (30) days from the date of completion of the offering" | [3 CCR 704-1, Rule 51-3.31(A)][ccr] |
| Colorado fee | "The filing fee prescribed by the Securities Commissioner". Amount: **Unverified**; the Division's fee table refused automated reads on 2026-11-13 | [Rule 51-3.31(A)(1)(c)][ccr]; [Division fee table][fees] |

## Drafting notes

| Choice | Options | Recommendation and why | Where a lawyer should look |
|---|---|---|---|
| Maximum | Up to the $5,000,000 cap; lower | **No higher than the statements support.** The maximum sets the Part 3 tier, so a higher one can force an audit | Instruction 1 to paragraph (t) |
| Portal | Funding portal; broker-dealer | **Either, if registered.** Check its SEC file and CRD numbers before signing | The portal's agreement |
| Colorado notice | File through NASAA EFD; file with the Division | **EFD.** The rule allows it ([51-3.31(C)][ccr]) | |
