**Never:** first-person feelings or opinions, a claim of a professional relationship, a promise about an outcome, anything presented as tax advice, or an instruction to hide that AI was used.

## A7. Privilege and confidentiality

**On first use, and at the top of any case room:**

> **Not private like talking to your lawyer.** Legallas isn't a lawyer, and what you tell it, or tell an AI connected to it, isn't privileged just because it's about legal matters. Courts disagree on how far AI work is protected. One held that a person's chats with a public AI weren't protected at all, even after he gave them to his lawyer. Others protected some AI work done to prepare for a case.
> - Don't paste your lawyer's advice into an AI unless your lawyer has told you to. It can lose protection.
> - Don't put the other side's confidential documents into an AI until you've checked the court's protective order.
> - If a dispute is possible, ask your lawyer how to use AI **before** you start, and don't delete anything.

**Before connecting a consumer AI plan** (Claude Free, Pro, Max or Ultra), tick required, rebuilt each release from the vendor's pages:

> **Before you connect this plan**
> Passages from your documents will go to Anthropic and sit in your chat history. This plan isn't under a business contract.
> - **Training:** Anthropic may use your chats to train its models unless you turn that off in your privacy settings. Its pages say raw connector content is left out, but what you paste, and answers that repeat your documents, may not be.
> - **How long:** A chat stays until you delete it, then is removed within 30 days. With training on, chats may be kept for up to 5 years. Flagged chats may be kept for up to 2 years, safety scores for up to 7, and rating an answer can keep the whole chat for 5 years.
> - **Who else:** Anthropic may disclose data to comply with law or legal process. Courts have ordered AI companies to produce user chats and have allowed subpoenas to AI providers.
> - **Legal protection:** One federal court held a person's chats with consumer Claude weren't privileged. It added in a footnote that putting a lawyer's advice into them would give up protection for that advice.
> - **Court orders:** Some protective orders forbid putting the other side's confidential documents into AI tools like this one.
> - **This plan can't be zero-retention.** A case room set to "zero retention only" will refuse it.
> Last checked: [date]. [Anthropic's pages]
